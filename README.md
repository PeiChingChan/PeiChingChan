## Featured Research

### 🟠 Bacterial Cellulose Active Packaging Film (MS Thesis, NTU)
- **Idea:** Combine two microbial products into one material: foaming bacterial cellulose made by *Komagataeibacter*, and the natural antioxidant pigment canthaxanthin produced by engineered *E. coli*.
- **What I did:** Designed and carried out the entire project independently, from fermentation and pigment extraction to film fabrication, material characterization (SEM, FTIR, XRD, TGA), antioxidant and cytotoxicity testing, and a 15-day refrigerated fish storage trial.
- **Outcome:** The pigment-loaded film showed clearly higher antioxidant activity than plain cellulose and was non-toxic to cells. On refrigerated fish, it slowed bacterial growth and early-stage fat oxidation, a proof of concept for natural active packaging.
- **Next step I identified:** Raise pigment concentration and improve how it binds to the film, so the protective effect is stronger and lasts longer.
